## Evidence standard

A strong project should ideally contain:

1. A clearly stated objective.
2. Reproducible implementation.
3. Appropriate metrics.
4. Visual evidence.
5. Interpretation of results.
6. Limitations.
7. A clear evidence classification.

## Evidence classification

- **Professional:** derived from real professional/client work, with confidential information removed.
- **Academic/Research:** developed for academic or research purposes.
- **Synthetic Portfolio:** independently created using simulated or public-style data.
- **Teaching:** developed as instructional or mentoring material.

Synthetic work is never presented as client impact.
