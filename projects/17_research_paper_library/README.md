# Research Paper Library Organization

**Professional research-support case study**

## Objective
Organize a research-paper library into a consistent, searchable structure while preserving useful metadata and concise summaries.

## Workflow
1. Inspect source documents.
2. Extract identifying metadata.
3. Standardize filenames.
4. Categorize papers by topic.
5. Record concise research summaries.
6. Identify duplicates or near-duplicates.
7. Produce a structured index.
8. Create visual or summary outputs where required.

## Quality controls
- Preserve original paper identity.
- Avoid inventing metadata.
- Flag uncertain classifications.
- Maintain consistent naming conventions.
- Keep source documents separate from generated summaries.

## Demonstrated capability
Document organization, research synthesis, information retrieval, metadata quality control and technical summarization.

> Client research libraries and copyrighted PDFs are not published.
