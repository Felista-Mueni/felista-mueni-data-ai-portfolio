# CASP Critical Appraisal — Case-Control Study

**Professional/academic research-support case study**

## Objective
Demonstrate structured critical appraisal of a case-control study using the CASP Case Control Study Checklist.

## Appraisal dimensions
- Appropriateness of the study design
- Selection of cases and controls
- Measurement of exposure
- Identification and handling of confounding
- Precision and validity of results
- Interpretation of associations
- Applicability of findings

## Evidence workflow
1. Identify the study design.
2. Assess methodological validity.
3. Examine participant selection.
4. Evaluate exposure and outcome measurement.
5. Assess confounding and bias.
6. Examine reported effect estimates and precision.
7. Assess applicability.
8. Produce balanced strengths, limitations and conclusions.

## Demonstrated capability
This work demonstrates critical appraisal, evidence interpretation, academic writing and methodological reasoning.
