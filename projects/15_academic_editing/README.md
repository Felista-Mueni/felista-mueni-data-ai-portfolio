# Academic & Technical Editing — Evidence Case Study

**Professional writing / research-support work**

## Scope
Academic and technical editing focused on improving clarity, structure, methodological accuracy, consistency and evidence-based presentation.

## Typical workflow
Draft review → requirements check → factual/methodological consistency → structure → language editing → references → final quality review.

## Quality controls
- Preserve the author's intended meaning.
- Check internal consistency between results and conclusions.
- Check citations and reference formatting.
- Remove unsupported claims.
- Improve clarity without fabricating evidence.
- Verify that requested word limits and formatting requirements are respected.

## Demonstrated capability
This evidence complements the research, systematic-review and critical-appraisal projects in the portfolio.
