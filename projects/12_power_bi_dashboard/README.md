# Power BI Executive Dashboard — Evidence Framework

**Portfolio / Business Intelligence**

## Objective
Demonstrate how retail transactions can be transformed into an executive performance dashboard.

## Dashboard pages
1. Executive Summary — Revenue, Profit, Orders, Customers, Margin and trend.
2. Sales Performance — Region, category, monthly trend and top products.
3. Customer Analytics — Segments, contribution and average order value.
4. Profitability — Profit by category, margin by region and low-margin products.

## DAX evidence
Demonstrate aggregation, ratios, filtered calculations, distinct counts, time intelligence and percentage-of-total measures.

## Evidence standard
Screenshots should show working visuals, KPI cards and interactive filters. Synthetic dashboards must be labelled as synthetic.

> Confidential client dashboards are not published.
